Registered Nurse – Cardiac Intensive Care Unit

Lakeside Medical Center (fictional) is seeking an experienced Registered Nurse for our 20-bed Cardiac ICU.

Key Responsibilities
- Deliver patient care to critically ill cardiac patients, including post-operative and mechanically ventilated patients.
- Perform hemodynamic monitoring and titrate vasoactive medications.
- Educate patients and families on care plans and discharge.
- Precept new nurses and participate in unit committees.

Minimum Qualifications
- Current RN license.
- At least 2 years of critical care experience.
- BLS and ACLS certification required.
- Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN).

Preferred Qualifications
- CCRN certification.
- Experience with Epic EHR.
- Cardiac surgery ICU experience.
