Junior Data Analyst

Brightwater Health Trust is looking for a Junior Data Analyst to join our analytics team in Manchester.

Responsibilities:
• Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI for operational teams.
• Clean, model and analyse data from multiple sources using SQL.
• Present findings to non-technical stakeholders.
• Support ad-hoc analysis requests from clinical leads.

Essential criteria:
• Strong SQL skills.
• Experience with Excel, including pivot tables.
• Excellent communication skills and the ability to explain data to non-specialists.
• A degree in a numerate subject (mathematics, statistics, economics or similar).

Desirable criteria:
• Experience with Python and pandas.
• Knowledge of statistics and A/B testing.
• Experience with Tableau.

What we offer
Pension scheme, 27 days annual leave, hybrid working.
